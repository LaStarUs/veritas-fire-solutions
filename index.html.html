Independent Technical Review

Integrity. Independence.

Overview

Independent technical review of proposed fire remediation works, specifications and contractor quotations to ensure recommendations are proportionate, compliant and supported by appropriate evidence.

  • Assessment of proposed remedial scope
  • Evaluation of specification suitability
  • Review of contractor quotations and cost allowances

  • Assessment of compliance with relevant guidance and test evidence
  • Independent recommendations for optimisation and value

Independent Verification Inspection

Integrity. Independence.

Overview

Independent inspection and verification of completed fire remediation works to provide assurance that the agreed scope has been delivered and that works have been completed to an acceptable standard.

Verification Includes

  • Inspection of completed remedial works
  • Assessment against agreed scope and specifications
  • Review of workmanship and installation quality
  • Identification of outstanding defects or non-conformities
  • Verification report with photographic evidence
  • Recommendations for corrective actions where required

Client Benefit

Obtain independent assurance that remediation works have been completed correctly and that residual compliance risks have been identified before project sign-off.

Experience

Built on Practical Experience

Independent advice is strengthened by practical experience.

With over 17 years in passive fire protection, fire remediation and compliance, and as an Associate of the Institution of Fire Engineers (AIFireE), my career has progressed from hands-on installation and site supervision through project management to providing independent fire remediation and compliance advice.

Having worked throughout the fire remediation process has given me a practical understanding of how passive fire protection systems are installed, managed, inspected and verified. Today, I apply that experience to provide independent advice that is practical, evidence-based and focused on helping clients make informed decisions.
